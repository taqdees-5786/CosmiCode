Bright and Warm

Introducing SUN

The Sun is at the center of our Solar System. It is a giant ball of hot gas that gives us:

  • ☀️ Light
  • ⚡ Energy
  • 🌍 Warmth for life on Earth

Without the Sun there would be no life on Earth.

Surface Temperature

5,500°C

Size

Approximately 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun.

The Inner Planets

The 4 Rocky Planets

Mercury

  • Closest planet to the Sun
  • Smallest planet
  • A year lasts only 88 Earth days
☀️ Closest Planet

Venus

  • Hottest planet
  • Thick atmosphere traps heat
  • Spins backwards compared to most planets
🔥 Hottest Planet

Earth

  • Our home planet
  • Only known planet with life
  • 71% covered by water
🌍 Our Home

Mars

  • Known as the Red Planet
  • Has the largest volcano in the Solar System
  • Scientists hope humans may visit one day
🔴 Red Planet

The Outer Planets

Jupiter

  • Largest planet
  • Has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot
  • More than 90 moons

  • Famous for its rings

  • Could float in water if a giant ocean existed

  • Blue-green color comes from methane gas
